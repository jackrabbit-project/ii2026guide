The Event

48th ASFA International Invitational, an ASFA All-Breed Lure Trial.

Saturday, October 24 & Sunday, October 25, 2026

Couch Ranch, 24512 S. 4350 Rd., Vinita, OK 74301 (Region 4)

Permission granted by ASFA for the holding of this event under ASFA rules and regulations. Scheduling Committee Chair: Samantha Duryee.

Trial Hours

Trial hours (Central Daylight Time): 5:30 AM until completion.

Roll call & inspection: promptly at 6:00 AM. ALL DOGS, NO EXCEPTIONS.

Workers' hounds accepted for early inspection upon arrival, beginning one half hour before roll call.

Stakes split into tentative flights Friday, October 23 at 5:30 PM at the field.

Eligibility (Overview)

To be eligible, a hound must have, prior to the close of entries (25 September):

  • Earned at least 20 lifetime ASFA points (or equivalent in its country of residence); or
  • Be an AKC (or equivalent foreign) Field Champion of Record in another lure-coursing sanctioning organization; or
  • Have placed in a Regional Invitational (with competition).

Singles and LCI entrants must have earned 20 lifetime ASFA points. Entrants in breed stakes must have earned the 20 points running against competition in their specific breed's stake; points earned in Singles do not count toward breed-stake eligibility. Provisional Stake entries need one 1st placement or two 2nd placements (with competition) in the Provisional Stake.

Full eligibility, registry and proof requirements are in the Stakes tab and the official premium PDF.

Stakes

Regular Stakes Offered

Open: Any eligible sighthound that has met certification requirements and earned 20 lifetime ASFA points (or equivalent / FCh of record elsewhere / placed in a Regional Invitational) prior to the close of entries. Hounds that have earned an ASFA Field Championship are not eligible for Open.

Field Champion (FCh): Any ASFA Field Champion.

Veteran: Any hound eligible for Open or FCh that meets the breed age minimum: 6 yrs (default), 5 yrs for Irish Wolfhounds, and 7 yrs for Afghan Hounds, Ibizan Hounds, Rhodesian Ridgebacks, Salukis & Whippets.

Provisional: Any sighthound of an eligible provisional breed with one 1st placement or two 2nd placements (with competition) in the Provisional Stake. Not eligible for Best of Breed or Best in Field.

Singles: Any hound otherwise eligible for regular/provisional stakes, including hounds with breed or competition disqualifications. Runs alone twice. Not eligible for BOB or BIF. Eligible to earn TCP and CPS titles (provisional breeds excluded from these titles).

Lure Chasing Instinct (LCI) Stake

Open to non-sighthounds and sighthound mixes that have earned 20 lifetime ASFA points. Must be individually registered (AKC incl. ILP/PAL, UKC, or ASFA number).

Non-Regular Stakes (LCI & Singles not eligible)

Breeder Stake: Two hounds bred or co-bred by the same individual. Both must be entered in a regular stake (Open, FCh, Veteran). Combined scores; highest wins the Breeder Stake Award.

Kennel Stake: Two hounds owned and living with the same person. Both must be entered in a regular stake. Combined scores; highest wins.

Generation Stake: A stud dog or brood bitch plus one offspring of that dog. Both entered in a regular stake (need not be owned/bred by the same person). Each parent pairs with one chosen offspring; an offspring may pair with one or both parents.

Bench Champion Stake: For any hound eligible for a regular stake that is an AKC, CKC, or ASFA-approved international show champion of record. A copy of the championship certificate must accompany each entry. ASFA does not recognize the UKC show champion as of Jan. 1, 2022.

For Breeder & Kennel: entries may be the identical pairing, but each dog may only be entered once in each stake. Hounds are named at the time of entry.

Gillette Stake (Saturday only)

Run following Best-in-Field procedures. The eligible hound in each breed with the highest qualifying combined score in the Field Champion or Veteran Stake on Saturday is randomly drawn to run in the Gillette Stake. Run on Field B; no additional entry fee.

Eligibility: a hound must have ALL of:

  • Be an AKC, ISWS, or foreign bench champion of record;
  • Be an ASFA Field Champion; and
  • Be entered in the Field Champion or Veteran Stake on Saturday.

Requirements must be met prior to close of entries; proof of bench title (photocopy) must accompany the entry, no exceptions. A separate Gillette entry form must reach the FTS by the regular closing date. ASFA does not recognize the UKC as a foreign-based registry for Bench Champion (as of Jan. 1, 2022).

Best in International Invitational (Sunday only)

Run Sunday following Best-in-Field procedures. All Best of Breed winners from Saturday and Sunday are eligible, provided they were entered and received qualifying scores running in prelims and finals both days.

The winner of Best in International Invitational is credited with winning a Best in Field.

Eligible Breeds

Regular stakes (purebred): Afghan Hound, Azawakh, Basenji, Borzoi, Cirneco dell'Etna, Greyhound, Ibizan Hound, Irish Wolfhound, Italian Greyhound, Peruvian Inca Orchid, Pharaoh Hound, Rhodesian Ridgeback, Saluki, Scottish Deerhound, Silken Windhound, Sloughi, Whippet.

All entries must be individually registered with AKC (ILP/PAL included), NGA, FCI, UKC, an ASFA-recognized foreign / ASFA-approved registry, or possess a CRN from SPDBS.

Substitutions & Stake Changes

Changes of stake and substitutions for lame or in-season hounds may be made by notifying Jennifer Gysler (Jennifer.DogShowEntries@gmail.com).

Refunds

100% of entry fees are refunded if a written request reaches the Trial Secretary on or before the trial closing date.

After the trial closes, a refund is given for an ill or injured dog or handler, bereavement, and/or family emergency, if the FTS is notified by the owner/handler prior to the close of roll call. Bitches in season are fully refunded on the same notice. No refund is given once a request is presented after the close of roll call.

No entry fee is refunded if the trial cannot open or be completed due to riots, civil disturbance, fire, act of God, public emergency, or other cause beyond the committee's control; nor if a dog is absent, disqualified, excused, or barred by action of the Field Trial Committee.

There is a $50 fee for returned checks.

Exhibitors should follow their veterinarian's recommendation to ensure dogs are free of parasites and communicable disease and have appropriate vaccinations.

Field Conditions

Average high temperature ~68°F. Natural vegetation.

Gas- or battery-powered continuous-loop machines will be used; the lure consists of white (and/or black if warranted) plastic strips. Backup equipment available.

Practice will NOT be offered at the event. The course is reversed for finals, tie run-offs and BOB.

The Field Committee reserves the right to alter the course plan based on weather and/or field conditions; significant changes will be posted.

Running Procedure

  • Stakes split into flights, in public view, at 5:30 PM Friday, 23 October, if any one stake has 20 or more hounds.
  • All hounds run twice, in trios if possible, or braces, unless excused, dismissed or disqualified.
  • Singles and LCI run alone twice in the trial.
  • Hounds not present at roll call are scratched after a five (5) minute grace period.
  • Bitches in season, hounds with breed disqualifications, and lame hounds at roll call / inspection are excused and entry fee refunded.
  • Monorchid, cryptorchid, spayed or neutered hounds may be entered. Muzzles are not required.
  • A gas/battery-powered continuous-loop lure machine is used on each field; backup equipment available. Lure: highly visible plastic strips.
  • The course is reversed for finals and run-offs at the judges' discretion.
  • $5 fine for any loose hound on the field not taking part in a course in progress.
  • No one except Field Committee members on the field without express permission of the Field Trial Chairman.

Notice to Exhibitors

The safety of the dog is our primary concern. By entering, exhibitors acknowledge they are familiar with the rules and regulations of this sport and that their dogs are able to compete safely.

Exhibitors alone are responsible for their own behavior and that of their dogs and/or children. Any exhibitor whose dogs or children create an unnecessary disturbance or repeatedly behave unsafely may, at the committee's discretion, be asked to leave the trial site with no refund. ASFA assumes no responsibility for any loss, damage or injury to exhibitors, handlers, dogs, property or children, and may decline entries for cause or remove any dog for aggression.

  • Unsportsmanlike conduct = excused from the field for the day.
  • The Field Committee may bar known aggressive hounds from the trial site.
  • $10.00 fine for anyone not picking up after their own dog(s).
  • $5 fine for any loose hound on the field not taking part in a course in progress.
